# Teach a robot how to make a PB & J sandwich

## Assumptions
- Fully charged humanoid robot
- There is a counter top with the following items:
    - Two slices of Bread on a plate
    - Jar of Jelly
    - Jar of Peanut Butter
    - Butter Knife


## Steps to Follow
1. Position self in front of items
2. Open the jar of peanut butter
3. Open the jar of jelly
4. Grab the butter knife
5. Scoop peanut butter with the butter knife
6. Pick up Bread and Spread Peanut butter on the flat surface of the bread 
7. Place Peanut butter bread on the plate with the peanut butter side facing up
8. Scoop jelly with the butter knife
9. Pick up the second slice of bread and spread jelly on the flat surface of the bread
10. Put down the butter knife
11. Place jelly bread on to the peanut butter bread with jelly face down
12. Replace lids on the jars
13. Put the jars and butter knife into their correct spots


## Done When
- When PB&J is created
- Work Station is cleaned
